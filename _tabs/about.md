# Juheon Kang
<p style="color: #6c757d; margin-top: -0.5rem;">강주헌</p>

**Ph.D. Student in Artificial Intelligence**  
<small style="color: #868e96;">University of Seoul (서울시립대학교)</small>

I am a graduate student researching **AI Safety**, **Model Interpretability**, and **Natural Language Processing**. With a background in Mathematics and Philosophy, I study how AI systems can operate safely, robustly, and transparently, and how these technologies can contribute meaningfully to society.

**Research Interests:** AI Safety & Alignment · Mechanistic Interpretability · LLM Evaluation & Red-Teaming · De-identification · Robustness
